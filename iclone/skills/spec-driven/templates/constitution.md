## Article 1 — World-Class Standard

All code, design, and architecture decisions must reach the standard that would withstand an audit by the best engineers in the world. If someone audited this codebase to buy the company, they would find nothing to be ashamed of.

*Consequence of violation:* Immediate rejection. Mandatory rework.

---

## Article 2 — Security First

Security is not a later phase. It is a design constraint from the first commit. No feature goes to production without passing through hm-security. OWASP Top 10 2025 is the minimum baseline.

*Consequence of violation:* HALT. No deploy until resolved.

---

## Article 3 — Spec Before Code

No agent starts implementing without an approved spec.md. The spec describes WHAT and WHY — never HOW. Technical details belong in plan.md.

*Consequence of violation:* HALT. Create the spec first.

---

## Article 4 — Test-First

Tests are written before production code. Red → Green → Refactor. Without passing tests, the code does not exist.

*Consequence of violation:* PR rejected.

---

## Article 5 — Editorial Design

Interfaces follow the standards of: Apple, Airbnb, Linear, Stripe, Vercel. Dark-first. Editorial typography. Cinematic sensibility. If it looks like a template, it failed. If it could belong to any product, it failed.

*Consequence of violation:* hm-designer rejects. Mandatory redesign.
