1. Who we are and what this covers

icloneOS is published by CLONE FRAME (cloneframe.io). In this policy, “we” and “us” mean CLONE FRAME, and “you” means the person using the app. For the personal data we receive, described in this policy, we are the controller.

This policy covers the icloneOS app (bundle identifier io.cloneframe.icloneos) on macOS, and the iPhone and iPad versions, which follow this same policy. Where something works differently on one platform, we say so. The policy also covers anything you send us directly, such as a bug report, an email or a message on X.

icloneOS is built on Hermes, the open-source agent by Nous Research, released under the MIT licence. The app gives each iNFT you hold its own agent. An iNFT is an NFT that carries an AI agent. The agent runtime and its data live on your device.

Third-party services you reach through the app, such as your AI provider, Privy, Virtuals and AgentCard, have their own policies. Section 19 links to them.

2. The short version

  • Your agent lives on your device. Its conversations, memory, persona and your files stay on your device. We run no servers that receive them.
  • You choose the AI provider. When your agent works, your messages and the context it needs go directly from your device to the provider you connected, under that provider’s terms. Nothing is sent to any AI provider until you connect one.
  • Sign-in uses Privy, with an email address or a wallet. We can see the account record Privy keeps for our app. Neither we nor the app ever see your wallet’s private keys.
  • The agent economy runs on accounts you open with other companies. The marketplace, agent email and trading views come from Virtuals, and virtual cards come from AgentCard. You open those accounts yourself, under their terms.
  • Blockchains are public. Wallet addresses, iNFT ownership and on-chain transactions can be seen by anyone, and they cannot be deleted.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling of data, and no analytics SDK in the app. The one exception to be aware of: the Privy and WalletConnect code in the sign-in window does its own event reporting to those companies.
  • You control deletion. You can delete an agent profile, sign out, remove everything the app stores, or ask us to delete your account.
  • icloneOS is for adults. You must be 18 or older to use it.

5. Third-party AI

What is sent

The agent’s reasoning is done by an AI model from a provider you choose. Each time the agent works, your device sends that provider:

  • your messages, and any files, images or attachments you give the agent;
  • the context the agent needs: its persona, its memory (which can include facts about you) and recent conversation;
  • the results of the agent’s tools, which can include web pages, command output, file contents, inbox messages and, if you allow screen recording, what is on your screen.

This is personal data shared with a third-party AI provider. It goes directly from your device to the provider. It does not pass through us, and we cannot see it.

It is your choice

icloneOS supports many providers (large commercial model providers, model routers, and model endpoints you run yourself). Nothing is sent to any of them until you connect one with your own API key or account. Connecting a provider is your explicit permission for the app to send it the data listed above whenever you ask your agent to work.

How to withdraw it

You can withdraw your permission at any time, and it takes effect immediately:

  • remove the provider’s key or sign out of it in the app’s settings;
  • switch to a different provider; or
  • point the agent at a model endpoint you run yourself, so that the data does not leave your control.

Withdrawing stops future sending. It does not recall what was already sent. The provider keeps that data under its own terms.

What the provider does with it

Your provider handles your data under its terms with you, including how long it keeps your data and whether it uses your data to train models. Many providers let you change these settings in your account with them. You pay the provider directly for what your key uses.

We do not use your conversations to train any model, and we never receive them.

AI output can be wrong. Do not paste secrets, such as passwords or recovery phrases, into a conversation: anything in the conversation is sent to your provider.

6. Accounts, sign-in and wallets

Signing in

You sign in through Privy with an email address or a wallet. No other sign-in methods are offered.

Sign-in happens in a separate, isolated window. That window can only load the app’s local sign-in page, and it refuses every request for device permissions.

After you sign in, the app holds your wallet address, your Privy user ID and an account label (your email address, or your wallet address) in memory. Privy’s code keeps your session in a storage area used only by the sign-in window, until you sign out.

What we can see

Privy keeps the account for our app. Through Privy, we can see the account record: your email address or wallet address, your Privy user ID and your embedded wallet address. We do not see your conversations, your files or your keys.

Wallets and keys

  • Your own wallet. If you sign in with a wallet you already have, its keys stay in that wallet.
  • Embedded wallet. If you sign in with an email address and have no wallet, Privy creates one for you. Its keys are secured by Privy, under Privy’s terms.
  • In both cases, neither the app nor CLONE FRAME ever has your private keys or recovery phrase. We cannot access, recover or move your assets.
  • We will never ask for your recovery phrase or private key. The sign-in page says the same: never enter a seed phrase or private key there.

Checking which iNFTs you hold

To find your iNFTs, the app sends your wallet address to a blockchain explorer (Blockscout) or a public blockchain node. It reads ownership only from known contracts on Base and Robinhood Chain. It then loads each iNFT’s files from the addresses in its on-chain metadata.

Virtuals sign-in

The agent economy uses a separate sign-in to Virtuals, done in your web browser. There you also approve a signing key for your agent. That key and the Virtuals sign-in token are stored in your macOS Keychain. Virtuals creates and manages the agent’s own wallet.

7. The agent economy

These features are optional. They run on the Virtuals Agent Commerce Protocol and on AgentCard, under accounts you open with those companies.

Marketplace

Your agent can browse the marketplace, publish offerings and prices, take on jobs and manage subscriptions. What you publish is sent to Virtuals and is meant to be seen by other marketplace participants, together with your agent’s identity.

Agent email

Each agent can have its own inbox, provided by Virtuals. It is the agent’s inbox, not your personal email account. Messages are stored by Virtuals. The app shows the inbox, and lets you and your agent read, search, write and reply.

The app keeps a preview of recent messages on your device, with verification codes masked. When your agent reads a message, the message becomes part of what is sent to your AI provider.

Virtual cards

You can set up a virtual payment card with AgentCard. Card issuing is backed by Stripe. During setup, you give AgentCard your first and last name, phone number and email address, and you set a spending limit. AgentCard and its partners may ask you for more information to meet their own legal obligations.

  • The app’s interface never shows full card numbers or security codes. They are removed before anything reaches the screen, and the interface cannot issue a card.
  • If you let your agent pay with a card from its own terminal, the card details it needs for that purchase pass through the agent, and so through your AI provider.

Trading views

The app can show your agent’s Hyperliquid positions and open orders, read through the Virtuals ACP tool, with links to a blockchain explorer. These views are read-only: the app does not place orders from its interface. Hyperliquid is an independent third party.

Who can move value

The app’s panels and buttons can only read and list. They cannot sign, move value or issue a card. Actions that sign or move value run in your agent’s terminal, where you can see them, and they work only with authorisations you gave yourself: the signing key you approved for Virtuals, and the card spending limit you set. See section 17.

8. Blockchain data is public and permanent

Blockchains are public records. Wallet addresses, iNFT ownership, transfers, payments and other transactions can be seen by anyone, at any time. They cannot be changed or deleted, by us or by anyone else, and this does not change if you delete your account.

A wallet address can be linked to your identity: for example, through a payment or a public profile, or because you share it. On-chain records are kept by the networks themselves, not by us.

10. What we never do

  • We show no ads.
  • We do not track you across other companies’ apps and websites, and we do not use the advertising identifier. The app does not use App Tracking Transparency because it does no tracking.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • We put no analytics, telemetry or crash-reporting SDK in the app. Crash information stays in a local log.
  • We do not receive your conversations, your agent’s memory or your files.
  • We do not give data to data brokers or build profiles of you.

The code that Privy and WalletConnect provide for the sign-in window includes their own event reporting, sent to those companies under their policies. It runs only in the sign-in window. We disclose it here and in the App Store privacy details.

Delete an iNFT’s agent profile

  1. Open the iNFT’s settings in the app.
  2. Choose to delete its profile. This removes that agent’s runtime profile and its persona from your device.
  3. If you also want to remove that iNFT’s cached wallet, market and inbox views, clear them as well; the app lets you clear them for each iNFT.

This does not affect the token on the blockchain, or the agent’s account with Virtuals.

Sign out

Signing out clears the Privy session from your device, along with the app’s list of your iNFTs. It does not delete your Privy account, your Virtuals sign-in, your agents’ local data or the cached views stored by the app.

Remove everything from your Mac

  1. In the app’s settings, open the uninstall section.
  2. Choose how much to remove: the app only; the app and the agent runtime, keeping your configuration, conversations and secrets; or everything, including conversations, secrets, scheduled jobs and logs.
  3. To finish a complete wipe, also delete the app’s folder in ~/Library/Application Support, the folder ~/.iclone-os, and, in the Keychain Access app, the entries saved by the Virtuals ACP tool.

On iPhone and iPad, deleting the app removes the data it stores on the device.

Delete your account with us

  1. Send a private report on github.com/devclone20/icloneos (on that page: Security, then “Report a vulnerability”; only you and we can read it), or use the form at cloneframe.io/#/bug. Include the email address or wallet address you sign in with. We may ask you to prove you control it, for example by signing a message. We never ask for your keys.
  2. We delete your Privy account record, and anything else we hold about you, within 30 days.
  3. We confirm by reply when the deletion is complete.

Deleting your account does not remove assets from your wallet. If you use a Privy embedded wallet, move any assets you want to keep before you ask for deletion, because we cannot recover the wallet afterwards.

Delete your accounts with other services

Your accounts with your AI provider, Virtuals, AgentCard, Nous Portal and any messaging platform are yours and separate from us. Delete them with each service (section 19).

What cannot be deleted

  • On-chain data (section 8).
  • Data a provider must keep by law. For example, card and identity records that AgentCard or Stripe are required to retain.
  • Data already sent to a service under your own account. That service’s policy applies.

Everyone can delete their account, wherever they live.

15. Security

We design the app so that the most sensitive things never reach it, and the rest stays under your control.

  • Private keys and recovery phrases are never in the app. Wallet keys stay in your wallet or with Privy.
  • The sign-in window is isolated from the rest of the app, can only load the local sign-in page, and refuses all device permission requests.
  • The Virtuals signing key and sign-in token are stored in your macOS Keychain.
  • Card numbers and security codes are removed before anything reaches the app’s screen. The interface cannot sign, move value or issue a card.
  • iNFT files are fetched only over HTTPS, with a size limit, and never from local or private network addresses.
  • App updates come from our release channel on GitHub, are signed and notarised for macOS, and are installed only after you agree.
  • The app’s own connections for sign-in, blockchain lookups, iNFT files and updates use HTTPS.

Where the app is weaker than it could be, here is exactly how:

  • AI provider keys and bot tokens you enter are stored in a plain-text file, ~/.hermes/.env. Your macOS account and disk encryption protect it, but any program running as your user can read it.
  • Connection secrets for remote gateways are in files only your user account can read. Encrypting them with your Keychain is an option you can turn on, and it is off by default.

We recommend you turn on FileVault, use provider keys with spending limits, and turn on Keychain encryption for connection secrets.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects personal data we hold, we will tell the people affected and the authorities where the law requires, without undue delay.

16. International transfers

The services in section 4 may process data in the United States and in other countries, which may not have the same data protection laws as yours.

When you send data to a service under your own account, such as your AI provider, Virtuals or AgentCard, the transfer happens at your direction, under that service’s terms.

For your Privy account, which Privy keeps for us, we rely on the transfer safeguards in Privy’s data protection terms. Where they apply, these include the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses and their UK equivalent.

17. Agent actions and automated decisions

Your agent is software that acts on your instructions, using the AI model you chose. It can browse, write, run commands, send agent email, take marketplace jobs and, if you set it up, spend within a card limit. What it does, it does for you.

We make no decisions about you by automated means: no profiling, and no decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

The limits are yours:

  • The app’s interface cannot sign transactions, move value or issue cards.
  • Signing and moving value need authorisations you gave yourself: the signing key you approved for Virtuals in your browser, and the spending limit you set on a card.
  • You can watch what the agent runs, stop it at any time, lower or remove limits, revoke the signing key with Virtuals, and sign out.

AI models make mistakes. Review what your agent does, especially anything that involves money.

Trading and crypto-assets are risky, and you can lose money. Wallets, cards, the marketplace and trading venues are provided by third parties under their own terms. You are responsible for your decisions and for what your agent does under the limits you set.
